How to play

Each guess fills the active horizontal row and vertical column. The crossing square is shared.

Use the colour and arrow clues to solve all five rows and all five columns. Correct squares stay green.

Green · exact
Yellow · row
Red · column
Orange · both
White · elsewhere
Black · absent

Arrow counts indicate how many copies of a letter occur in the relevant answer word. Duplicate letters matter.
