How to play

Solve all ten words: five across and five down.

  • Every guess is a five-letter word. It goes into the highlighted row and the highlighted column at the same time.
  • Each square collects a clue tile for every letter placed in it. Your latest guess shows full size for a few seconds, then shrinks into small history tiles so the board is clear for your next guess. Only green squares stay full size.
  • You start with 10 guesses. Solve a word and earn +1 guess, for your first 8 solved words.
  • Solved squares turn green and stay put. The row and column always share a number (row 2 with column 2, and so on); a pair is skipped only once both its row and column are solved.

Reading the tiles

STAREZ
  • Green — right letter in the right square.
  • Yellow — the letter is somewhere else in that square’s row word.
  • Red — the letter is somewhere else in that square’s column word.
  • Orange — it appears in both the row and the column word.
  • White — it’s in the grid, but not in that row or column. Black — it’s not in the grid at all.
  • Arrows show the direction: ◀ ▶ on the sides = row, ▲ ▼ top and bottom = column.
ESO
  • More arrow pairs mean more copies. Both the answer and your guess must contain the letter more than once; you get the smaller of the two counts.

Reading the keyboard

ABCDEFG
  • Keys update after every guess for the highlighted row and column.
  • Purple — not guessed yet. White — guessed, nothing more to say about this row/column.
  • Yellow / Red / Orange — more copies of this letter are still needed in the row / column / both.
  • Dark grey — can’t go anywhere else in either highlighted line. Black — not in the grid at all.

Handy tools

  • Tap a tile to fade it, tap again to hide it, once more to restore.
  • Press and hold a tile (or right-click) to highlight every tile with that letter. Tap a past guess to highlight its tiles.
  • Notes: turn it on, tap a square, then type a letter to pencil it in. Type it again to mark it certain.
